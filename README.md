# foldit-guided

**Move energetics for Foldit players:** an unofficial companion that shows which physical energy
term each Foldit move changed, which atoms are responsible, and whether Foldit's score agrees.

It is a static site with no install, account, server, build step, dependencies or tracking.
Structures are analysed and stored in the player's own browser.

> **Beta.** The capture recipe has been tested against a simulated Foldit Lua API, not yet inside the game.

## Use it with Foldit

Open https://scichemcode.github.io/foldit-guided/. The page walks you through these steps.

**Before you start:** use a Beginner or Science puzzle (Intro Puzzles have no Cookbook). Chrome or
Edge on a computer lets the page update by itself.

**Set up once**

1. **Download the recipe** from the page. You get `foldit-energy-capture.lua`.
2. **Take a first capture.** In Foldit, open the Cookbook, click *Load local recipe* (the folder icon
   at the bottom), choose the file, click *Capture*, and wait for "Capture finished".
3. **Connect the page.** Click *Choose the script log* and pick the file:
   - Windows: usually `C:\Foldit\scriptlog.default.xml`
   - Mac: usually in `/Applications/Foldit.app/Contents/Resources` (Cmd+Shift+G in the file chooser)
   - Linux: in the folder Foldit was started from
4. **Make one move and capture again.** The page says whether it helped, why, and what to try.

**Then repeat:** move, capture, read.

Keep the page open while you play: Foldit overwrites the log on every recipe run. In browsers other
than Chrome and Edge, choose the log again after every capture.

Every puzzle gets its own history. Don't share captures or exports of active puzzles, and follow the
[Foldit community rules](https://fold.it/community_rules).

## How it works

    Foldit + recipe ──print──> scriptlog.<track>.xml ──file picker──> page
                                                                       worker: rebuild, relax H, energies
                                                                       IndexedDB: history per puzzle

The recipe measures atom-to-anchor distances with temporary, disabled bands and deletes them. It
moves no atoms. The site rebuilds coordinates from those distances, rebuilds and relaxes hydrogens,
and evaluates Amber ff14SB + GBn2. It does not read or decode Foldit's save files.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `recipe.js` | the Lua recipe players paste into Foldit |
| `physics/capture.js` | distances to coordinates |
| `physics/topology.js` | ff14SB / GBn2 parameter assignment |
| `physics/forces.js`, `physics/minimize.js` | energies, gradients, hydrogen relaxation |
| `physics/analysis.js` | change per term and its explanation |
| `physics/params.js` | generated by `tools/build_params.py` (needs OpenMM; only to regenerate) |
| `guide.js`, `summary.js` | step-by-step guide, plain-language result |
| `app.js`, `store.js`, `watch.js`, `transfer.js` | page, storage, live log, backup |

## Limits

- 11 to 450 protein residues. Ligands, nucleic acids and symmetry partners are not modelled.
- Energies at cut points are not physical, and no ΔE is given when atoms or bonds change.
- These are potential energies of static structures: not free energies, not Foldit's Rosetta score.
- Time per capture on a desktop:

| Residues | 20 | 76 | 151 | 263 | 428 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seconds | 0.2 | 4 | 29 | 95 | 300 |

## Still to confirm inside Foldit

- Band atom indices follow Rosetta order (1=N, 2=CA, 3=C, 4=O, then the side chain).
- `band.GetLength` returns the exact atom-to-atom distance.
- `print` reaches the script log, and where that log lives on current installs.
- The current names of the Cookbook controls (*Load local recipe*, *Show recipe output*).
- The recipe is fast enough: it creates and deletes about 90 bands per residue.

Wrong numbering or distances are designed to fail the chemistry and consistency checks and show as an error.
