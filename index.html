Why did that move cost energy?

Each time you capture a move, this page splits the energy change into bond, angle, torsion, repulsion, dispersion, electrostatic and solvation terms. It then names the residues and atoms responsible and sets the result beside Foldit’s own score.

Every puzzle gets its own history. Press Show an example to see it without Foldit.

Privacy. Your captures and history stay in this browser. Clearing site data erases them.

Fair play. Don’t pass captures or exports of active puzzles to other players. Follow the Foldit community rules.

What the numbers are. Amber ff14SB + GBn2 potential energies of static structures: not free energies and not Foldit’s Rosetta score. “Disagree” means the two functions differ, not that either is wrong. Proteins of 11–450 residues (about 4 s at 76 residues, 30 s at 150, 5 minutes at 430); ligands, nucleic acids and symmetry partners are not modelled.
